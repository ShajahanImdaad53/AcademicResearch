# Questionnaire Draft
**Note: Do not distribute this questionnaire until institutional ethics approval is obtained.**

## Part 1: Academic Profile & Subjects
1. What is your current year of study? (Categorical: Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, Year 4, Postgraduate)
2. Please list up to 5 subjects you are currently studying.
*(For each subject listed, the following questions apply)*
3. On a scale of 1-5, how difficult do you find this subject? (1 = Very Easy, 5 = Very Difficult)
4. What was your previous academic performance band in this or a similar subject? (Categorical: High Distinction/A, Distinction/B, Credit/C, Pass/D, Fail/F, N/A)

## Part 2: Deadlines and Workload
5. How many days until your next major assessment or exam for this subject? (Numeric)
6. What is the weighting of this upcoming assessment? (Numeric: 0-100%)

## Part 3: Availability and Preferences
7. How many total hours are you available to study this week? (Numeric)
8. What is your preferred study session duration? (Options: 30 mins, 45 mins, 60 mins, 90 mins, 120 mins)
9. What is your preferred break duration between sessions? (Options: 5 mins, 10 mins, 15 mins, 20 mins, 30 mins)
10. Are there specific days or time windows you are completely unavailable? (Free text/calendar selection)

## Part 4: Target Variable (Priority Assessment)
11. Based on your inputs above, how would you classify the priority of studying [Subject Name] this week? (Select one: Low, Medium, High)

## Part 5: System Feedback (For Post-Intervention Evaluation)
12. Did the generated schedule reflect a realistic study plan? (Likert scale 1-5)
13. How useful did you find the AI recommendations for prioritizing your time? (Likert scale 1-5)
